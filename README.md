## About Me

I am an **Information Technology student at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences in Finland**, specializing in **Machine Learning**.

My current focus is on building a strong foundation in **machine learning, neural networks, data science, data engineering and software engineering**. I enjoy understanding how ML systems work from the fundamentals and then applying those concepts to practical problems.

My background also includes **full-stack development, backend systems, databases, DevOps, and system design**, giving me a broader software engineering foundation for building end-to-end ML applications.

Before moving into software engineering, I also gained professional experience in **research, data collection, transcription, translation, and analysis**, which helped develop my analytical and problem-solving skills.
